FAQs

Good to know

How do I book a room?{{icon:plus}}

Book online on Marriott.com or call the hotel on {{site.phone}}. Booking through Marriott lets you earn Marriott Bonvoy® points.

Is parking free?{{icon:plus}}

Yes, parking at the hotel is complimentary.

Do you have a pool?{{icon:plus}}

Yes, we have a saltwater indoor pool, open all year round. Please ask the front desk for current pool hours.

Can you host a meeting or event?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. Our meeting room holds up to about 80 guests. Send us an enquiry with your date and numbers and we’ll get back to you.

Do you offer group or corporate rates?{{icon:plus}}

Yes, discounted group and corporate rates are available. Contact the hotel with your dates and number of rooms.
